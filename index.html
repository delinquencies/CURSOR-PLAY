How the work is made

01

Listen

We begin with what the piece must do in the world—comfort, challenge, dedicate, mark a threshold. No template; only listening.

02

Draft in ink

First drafts stay on paper: slower lines, fewer clever escapes. The goal is sound before polish.

03

Revise in daylight

Cuts, bridges, and the occasional stubborn stanza that refuses to leave until it earns its room.
